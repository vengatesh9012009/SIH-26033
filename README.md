## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

## Impact and Benefits
• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
#### 1.**Group together.** Farmers form FPOs or co-ops to sell in bulk and get better prices, like Amul.
#### 2.**Sell directly.** Use e-NAM, farmer markets, or deal straight with shops and processors.
#### 3.**Store and add value.** Warehouses, cold storage, sorting and packing let farmers wait for a better price.
#### 4.**Get easy loans and fair rules.** Kisan Credit Cards, quick payment and open auctions keep farmers free from middlemen.
#### 5.**In one line:** Group up, sell directly, store and process, and use fair credit and markets

## Technical Approach
#### Farmer app. Works in local languages, even offline. Farmers add their crop, amount and photos.
#### Buyer site. Shops, processors and restaurants browse crops and place orders or bids.
#### Live prices. Show today’s market rates from Agmarknet and e-NAM, and predict demand.
#### Photo grading. The app checks crop photos and gives a quality grade.
#### Safe payment. The buyer pays by UPI, and the money is released when the crop is delivered.
#### Build and start small. Use Flutter or React Native, FastAPI or Node.js, and PostgreSQL. Test with one crop, one farmer group and one district, then grow.

## Feasibility and Viability
#### The app is the easy part. Anyone can build it.
#### Moving the crops is hard. Pickup, storage and delivery take the most work.
#### Profit on each sale is small. So you need a lot of sales to earn well.
#### Both sides need something. Farmers want better prices, and buyers want a steady supply.
#### Start small. Pick one crop, one area and one farmer group, and include delivery from day one, not just the app.

## Impact and Benefits
#### 1.Farmers earn more and get paid faster, with less produce wasted.
#### 2.Consumers get fresher food at fairer prices.
#### 3.Buyers get steady supply, and rural areas see more income and less dependence on middlemen.

## Research and References
#### Farmers get only about one-third of the price consumers pay for tomato, onion and potato.
#### Dairy farmers get about 70%, because milk has fewer middlemen.
#### Selling directly (like Uzhavar Sandhai) gave farmers 15-40% more than wholesale prices.
#### India has set up 10,000 farmer groups (FPOs) with government money and loans.
